Gaussian Splats

The models are trained with SteadySplats' training algorithm, which lowers the noise of stochastic sampling, and most of them with Mip-Splatting's 3D filter (with AAA-Gaussians' area normalization), which keeps them free of aliasing when zoomed out. Rendering is done by my own MSAA algorithm on top of StochasticSplats: sorting-free stochastic transparency, with temporal reuse while the camera moves and accumulation while it is still.

Note: because of a problem in Safari's WebGPU, 4x MSAA (quality) runs very slowly in Safari. Use the fast links there, or Chrome or Firefox for quality.

Each model opens in the WebGPU player: fast renders 1 sample per pixel, quality 4x MSAA. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, left/right for the dataset cameras.
